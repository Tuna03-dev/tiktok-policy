n8n_flow Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how n8n_flow collects, uses, stores, and protects data when used to generate, prepare, and publish short-form video content to an authorized TikTok account.

Information Collected

The workflow may process basic TikTok account information, TikTok access tokens, video files or video URLs, captions, hashtags, scripts, product information, publishing status, and workflow execution logs.

How Information Is Used

The information is used only to authenticate the authorized TikTok account, generate or prepare video content, upload drafts or publish videos through TikTok Content Posting API, track publishing status, and debug workflow errors.

Sharing of Information

n8n_flow does not sell personal data. Data may be sent to third-party services only when necessary to operate the workflow, such as TikTok APIs, Google Sheets, file storage, AI generation services, or video rendering services.

TikTok Data

TikTok account data and tokens are used only for actions authorized by the TikTok user. The workflow does not use TikTok data for advertising targeting, resale, profiling, or unauthorized sharing.

Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as needed to operate the workflow, troubleshoot issues, or maintain publishing history.
